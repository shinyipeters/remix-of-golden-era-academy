1. PROJECT OBJECTIVE



Build a complete, modern, professional, responsive website for Golden Era Digital Academy, a digital education and skills acquisition company providing training in cryptocurrency trading, forex trading, web design and development, graphic design, artificial intelligence, financial markets, social media production, and general digital skills.



The website must serve as the academy's official public platform, student registration and enrolment portal, payment management system, business communication channel, and administrative management system.



Appearing as: www.goldeneradigitalacademy.com



This must be a functional business website, not merely a visual prototype.



The founder must be able to manage the academy's website, courses, students, payments, announcements, gallery, contact details, and business enquiries through a secure administration dashboard after publication without routinely editing code or redeploying the website.



Use a persistent backend database and real integrations where required. Do not use localStorage as the primary storage system for critical business records.



Preserve all existing approved requirements and implement the features as one integrated system.

7. PAYMENT AND ENROLMENT SYSTEM



Create a functional payment and enrolment management system.



The owner must be able to configure:



- Course prices.

- Supported payment methods.

- Bank name.

- Account name.

- Account number.

- Payment instructions.

- Official payment contact channels.

- Payment reference requirements.



All payment information must be editable from the dashboard.



Payment workflow



1. A student selects a course.

2. The system displays the course description, price, and payment instructions.

3. The student submits an enrolment application.

4. The system generates a unique application reference.

5. The application is saved in the database.

6. The student selects an available payment method.

7. If using bank transfer, the student can submit a transaction reference and optional supporting evidence.

8. The payment enters a pending-verification state.

9. The owner receives a notification.

10. The owner reviews the submission.

11. The owner approves or rejects the payment.

12. The system records the decision and audit details.

13. The student's payment and enrolment statuses are updated accordingly.



Never mark a payment successful solely because the student uploaded a receipt, submitted a reference, or opened WhatsApp.



Only mark a payment verified after an authorised owner or administrator confirms it, or a properly integrated payment provider supplies a valid confirmation.



Payment records



Every payment record must include:



- Student name.

- Contact information.

- Selected course.

- Amount due.

- Amount submitted, where applicable.

- Payment method.

- Application reference.

- Transaction reference, if available.

- Submission date.

- Current payment status.

- Supporting evidence, where applicable.

- Administrative notes.

- Verification date.

- Verifying administrator.



Support statuses such as:



- Not Started.

- Awaiting Payment.

- Submitted for Verification.

- Verified.

- Rejected.

- Disputed.

- Refunded, where applicable.



If online payment processing is added, use a legitimate payment provider and its documented verification mechanisms.



Never store payment card security codes or unnecessary sensitive financial information.
